Signature Fonts

Fourteen script and handwriting fonts that make a typed name look signed. Preview them below, then type your own name in the generator and download it as a transparent PNG.

There are two families here, and the choice between them matters more than the details. Script fonts — Great Vibes, Pinyon Script, Alex Brush and friends — imitate a fountain pen doing something deliberate: these suit formal documents, certificates and business signatures. Handwriting fonts — Homemade Apple, Caveat — imitate an everyday pen moving fast: these suit personal notes, casual emails and anything that shouldn't look engraved.

How to sign with a font

  1. Pick a font from the gallery above and hit "Try it" — the signature generator opens with that font already selected.
  2. Type your name and adjust the size until it feels right.
  3. Download the PNG. It comes with a transparent background, cropped tight, at 2× resolution — ready for email signatures, PDFs and documents.

All 14 fonts are open-source Google Fonts, free for personal and commercial use. If you'd rather type with them directly, you can download and install them from Google Fonts — but for signed documents and email signatures, the PNG from the generator is what you actually need.

Frequently asked questions

Which signature font looks most like a real signature?

For a formal, fountain-pen look, Great Vibes, Alex Brush and Pinyon Script come closest to real signing. Sacramento and Allura feel lighter and quicker. Homemade Apple and Caveat look like everyday ballpoint handwriting.

Can I use these fonts in Word or Google Docs?

Yes. All 14 fonts are open-source Google Fonts, free for personal and commercial use. You can download and install them on your computer to type with them in any application, or use the SignSorted generator to turn a typed name into a PNG image.

Are these fonts free for commercial use?

Yes. Every font in the gallery is released under an open-source license (mostly the SIL Open Font License), which allows commercial use at no cost.

How do I turn my name into a signature image?

Open the signature generator, switch to the "Type it" mode, enter your name, pick a font from this gallery, and click Download PNG. You get a transparent-background image cropped tight around the ink.
