Frequently asked questions

Is SignSorted free to use?

Yes. Every tool on the site is free, with no account, no signup and no watermark. Create and download as many signatures as you like.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The tools run entirely in your browser, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

Is my signature stored on your servers?

No. Your signature is drawn, rendered and exported inside your browser. It is never uploaded, never stored and never seen by us.

Can I use the signature on official documents?

For everyday paperwork — forms, letters, invoices, school documents — a signature image is usually accepted. For documents that require a legally binding or certified electronic signature, you need a dedicated e-signature service that verifies identity; an image is not proof of who signed.

What can I do with the PNG I download?

Paste it into an email signature (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail), place it on a PDF or contract, insert it into Word or Google Docs, or use it on letters and cards. The transparent background keeps it clean on any page.
