Contact

One email address, read by a person. There is no contact form here — deliberately, because a form would mean storing what you type.

Email

hello@signsorted.com

Bug reports, feature requests and honest criticism all go to the same inbox. Nothing on this site stores submissions, so the email itself is the record.

If something is broken

The more of this you include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • The page URL — for example signsorted.com/signature-generator/.
  • Browser and device — Chrome, Safari or Firefox, on a laptop or a phone. Several bugs here only appear on one of those.
  • What you did and what happened instead — "the download button did nothing" is already a useful sentence; "it doesn't work" is not.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visible.

Please don't send a signature image, a password or anything else you'd mind sitting in an inbox. We never need it.

If you want a feature

Describe the thing you were trying to get done, not only the button you want. "I need a signature with my company's Chinese name underneath the English one" is a problem we can solve properly; "add a second text field" is one guess at the solution.

If a guide is wrong

Tell us the page and what you saw on your screen. Menu paths move between versions, and a wrong step costs someone an afternoon — corrections are made to the page itself and the date at the top changes with them.

Permission and reuse

The tools are free for personal and commercial use, and the images you make are yours to use however you like. If you want to republish the guides or bundle the tools into something else, ask first — the terms cover what is fine and what isn't.

What we can't help with

  • Recovering a signature you didn't download. Nothing is stored anywhere, so there is nothing to recover. Download and keep the PNG before you close the tab.
  • Legally binding or certified signatures. A drawn image is not a certified electronic signature, and we are not a certificate authority. If a document demands one, it needs a dedicated e-signature provider.
  • Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail account problems. Those are the provider's to solve — we only build the signature block you paste in.

How long a reply takes

This is a small project, and email is answered in batches. A bug that stops the tool working usually gets looked at first; a feature request may get a yes, a no, or a "not yet", but it gets read.
